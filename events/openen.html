The hotel has two swimming pools (outdoor and indoor) with mineral water, which use modern water purification systems such as hydrolysis.
Hydrolysis is a system that is designed to purify water, but without the use of chemicals such as bromine or chlorine.
In the hydrolysis process, water is broken down into hydrogen and oxygen. Oxygen is a powerful disinfectant that does not irritate the skin and has no odor.
When you use water that has been cleaned in other ways, you may get skin or eye irritation. With hydrolysis this is impossible.
